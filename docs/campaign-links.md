# Campaign links

Use lowercase UTM source, medium and campaign values. Example:

https://zipruschemicals.com/kaolin?utm_source=nairaland&utm_medium=forum&utm_campaign=kaolin_nigeria

Sources: nairaland, facebook, instagram, linkedin, google_business, youtube. Use forum, organic_social, or organic_local as appropriate. Do not include customer names, phone numbers or emails in UTM values.

Attribution stays in memory during client-side navigation and is submitted with enquiries to Ziprus OS. Reloading or opening a new tab can lose attribution. No analytics cookies are set by this feature.
